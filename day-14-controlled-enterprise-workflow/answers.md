# Answers

## What is Flow Builder?

Flow Builder is Salesforce's declarative automation tool used to automate business processes.

---

## What are the main Flow elements?

- Screen
- Assignment
- Decision
- Loop
- Create Records
- Update Records
- Delete Records
- Get Records

---

## What is a Decision Element?

A Decision Element evaluates conditions and determines which path a flow should follow.

---

## What is an Approval Process?

An Approval Process automates the approval of records by routing them to designated approvers.

---

## What happens when a record is submitted for approval?

The record is sent to an approver who can approve or reject it based on business requirements.

---

## What are Approval Steps?

Approval Steps define who approves a record and under what conditions.

---

## Benefits of Flow Builder?

- No-code automation
- Faster development
- Improved efficiency
- Reduced manual effort

---

## Benefits of Approval Processes?

- Standardized approvals
- Better compliance
- Automated notifications
- Improved tracking
