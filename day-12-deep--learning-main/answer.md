# Answers

## What is Salesforce DX?

Salesforce DX is a modern development framework that enables source-driven development on the Salesforce platform.

---

## What is Salesforce CLI?

Salesforce CLI is a command-line tool used to create projects, manage orgs, deploy metadata, and automate development tasks.

---

## What is a Scratch Org?

A scratch org is a temporary Salesforce environment used for development and testing.

---

## What are the benefits of Salesforce DX?

- Source-driven development
- Team collaboration
- Version control integration
- Faster deployments
- Automated workflows

---

## What is an Org Development Model?

An approach used to organize development, testing, and deployment activities across Salesforce environments.

---

## What is Agentforce DX?

Agentforce DX is a Salesforce development experience designed for building, testing, and managing AI-powered agents.

---

## Why is version control important?

Version control helps track changes, collaborate with teams, and manage application releases.

---

## What is metadata deployment?

Metadata deployment is the process of moving Salesforce components between environments.
